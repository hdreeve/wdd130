Abidjan Ivory Coast Temple

The Abidjan Ivory Coast Temple is located in the Cocody suburb of Abidjan. It features a beautiful steeple topped with a palladium-leafed statue of the angel Moroni. Its landscaped grounds include flowers, walkways, and a gathering plaza.
