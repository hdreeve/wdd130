Hayden Dee Reeve | WDD 130

My name is Hayden Reeve, and I live in a small town called Hooper, Utah. I am deaf and have used a hearing aid since I was two years old. I am taking WDD 130 because I have always been fascinated by technology, and I enjoy learning how to code and develop websites. I served my mission in Houston, Texas, which helped me grow personally and brought me closer to Jesus Christ.

In my free time, I enjoy going on hikes and exploring forests and nature. I also like playing volleyball with friends. Another thing I enjoy is serving as an American Sign Language ordinance worker at the Ogden Utah Temple.

I hope to use my programming skills to create designs, develop games, build websites, and explore other areas of technology. A friend of mine recently suggested collaborating on a project where we would work together to create a game. I think it would be a great opportunity to learn, gain experience, and put my programming skills into practice.
